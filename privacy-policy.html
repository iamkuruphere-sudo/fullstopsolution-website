LEGAL & PRIVACY COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2026 | Effective Date: Immediately

1. Zero-Knowledge Client-Side Architecture

At Fullstop Solution (accessible at https://www.fullstopsolution.in), privacy is our founding principle. All our free online utilities—including the Password Generator, JSON Formatter, Hash Calculator, Meta Tag Generator, Invoice Maker, and GST Calculator—execute 100% inside your web browser’s local client runtime.

Your input strings, passwords, cryptographic keys, customer names, financial figures, and JSON code are NEVER transmitted to or stored on our servers. When you close or refresh your browser tab, in-memory data is purged immediately.

2. Information We Collect During Commerce Transactions

When you purchase premium digital toolkits (such as our Business OS or SaaS Boilerplates), we collect minimal transactional information necessary for legal fulfillment:

  • Contact Information: Full name and email address to dispatch product download links and generated license keys.
  • Payment Processing: Transactions are processed securely by certified PCI-DSS compliant third-party gateways (Razorpay and PayPal). Fullstop Solution never sees or stores full credit card numbers or UPI MPINs.
  • Tax / Billing Records: Retained in compliance with Indian GST laws and international e-commerce accounting standards.

3. Compliance with India DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR

We adhere strictly to India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You retain full rights to request access to your transaction records, request deletion of your purchase email, or revoke marketing consent at any time.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use invasive tracking or cross-site tracking cookies. We utilize browser localStorage strictly for user experience preferences:

  • Your preferred color scheme (Dark or Light mode).
  • Your active product license keys so you can re-access purchased downloads seamlessly.
