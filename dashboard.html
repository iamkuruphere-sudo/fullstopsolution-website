🔍 Why couldn't you find 'fullstopsolution.in' in Google Analytics?

Google Analytics (GA4) does not automatically discover new domains until you create a Web Data Stream inside your Google Analytics account. Once created, Google generates a unique Measurement ID (formatted as G-XXXXXXXXXX) that connects your website to Google's reporting servers.

➕ Open GA4 Stream Creator ↗
Step 1: In Google Analytics
Click Admin (gear icon bottom-left) → Create Property named "Fullstop Solution".
Step 2: Add Web Stream
Under Data Streams, select Web. Enter URL: https://www.fullstopsolution.in.
Step 3: Paste Measurement ID
Copy the generated G-XXXXXXXXXX and paste it in the box below to activate instant tracking!
